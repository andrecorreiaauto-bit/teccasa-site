# Imagens das páginas de instalação por tipo

Criadas em 2026-11-02 com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen.imagegen` (modo built-in, sem CLI/API externa). São ilustrações de categorias de portão, não fotografias de instalações realizadas pela TecCasa nem esquemas de montagem. Esta distinção também consta da legenda pública.

Originais de 1536 × 1024 píxeis. Ficheiros publicados em WebP, em 1440 × 960 e 768 × 512, com `srcset`, dimensões explícitas e proporção preservada. Diretório no projeto: `public/automatismos/tipos/`.

## Prompts finais

### Basculante

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape hero illustration for a Portuguese residential garage automation installation webpage. Create a realistic architectural photograph, wide 3:2 composition, of ONE SINGLE RIGID PANEL up-and-over garage door (Portuguese portão basculante), partially open at about 45 degrees. Critical geometry: the entire rectangular door leaf is ONE straight flat uninterrupted steel slab, vertical ribs only, tilting up as a whole; NO horizontal articulated panels, NO sectional curved sliding tracks. View three-quarter from inside an ordinary tidy Portuguese garage looking towards driveway in daylight, with upper lintel and entire tilted leaf visible. Show plausible paired side pivot lifting arms supporting the slab and a modest generic ceiling-mounted electric opener behind it, neatly installed, with a straight central rail and articulated pull arm attached to upper inside of the door. Door anthracite, off-white walls, ordinary concrete floor, modest home, not luxury showroom. Realistic scale, usable clearance, clearly legible single slab silhouette. This is a conceptual illustration of the door type, not a step-by-step technical diagram. No people, no vehicles, no loose cables, no logos, no words, no watermark. Natural soft daylight, slightly warm neutral textures, restrained dark blue-grey materials, no dramatic glow.

### Seccionado

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape hero illustration for a Portuguese residential sectional garage door automation webpage. Realistic architectural photo, wide 3:2 composition, of an electrically operated SECTIONAL garage door partially raised, viewed three-quarter from inside a tidy modest Portuguese residential garage toward a quiet driveway. Critical distinction: clearly visible several HORIZONTAL hinged rigid panels; the panels follow matching curved side tracks from vertical jambs to horizontal tracks under the ceiling. Show a plausible modest generic ceiling motor with a straight central rail behind the open door, neat closed housing, no exposed wiring. White or light-grey door panels, natural white plaster walls, plain concrete floor, natural daylight. Frame the entire door opening, ceiling motor, central rail and the curve of the track to convey the type. Believable proportions and construction; illustration of category not installation instructions. No people, no cars, no logos, no lettering, no arrows, no measurements, no watermark.

### Correr

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape hero illustration for a Portuguese website about motorising an existing residential sliding gate. Realistic architectural photo in natural daylight, wide 3:2 composition. View from inside a modest Portuguese house's driveway toward a single anthracite steel SLIDING gate partially open, sliding parallel to a low white boundary wall. Gate is one rigid rectangular leaf with horizontal infill, supported on visible wheels and straight ground rail, adequate straight side run-back space beside wall. A compact generic sliding gate electric motor fixed to a small concrete plinth beside the stationary gate post meshes with a straight horizontal gear rack near the bottom of the moving leaf. Pair of modest safety photocells on opposite posts. Mechanism housings closed and neat, safe uncluttered driveway, believable physical assembly. Focus on gate and motor in foreground, whole gate fits frame. Calm everyday home, light plaster, restrained plants, not luxury. No persons, cars, brands, text, arrows, measurements or watermark.

### Batente

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape hero illustration for a Portuguese swing gate automation installation page. Realistic architectural photograph, wide 3:2 composition, taken from INSIDE a modest residential driveway. Two inward-opening anthracite steel swing gate leaves, one slightly more open than the other, mounted on sturdy off-white masonry pillars. Show a physically plausible linear electric actuator on the INSIDE of each gate leaf: rear bracket on its corresponding stationary pillar and front bracket part-way along the inside lower frame of its leaf; each actuator connects only its own pillar to its own leaf. Whole gate and both actuators visible, closed motor housings, tidy installation with no exposed electrical connections. Simple horizontal infill and enough inward swing space, quiet Portuguese street beyond. Natural soft daylight, normal lived-in home proportions, white plaster and grey stone paving, no luxury embellishments. No people, cars, logos, wording, dimensions, arrows or watermark.
